# 🛍️ Digital Product Hub

A modern digital marketplace where users can discover, purchase, and access high-quality digital products such as **UI Kits, Templates, Plugins, E-books, Courses, and Digital Assets**.

---

## 🌐 Live Demo

🚀 **Live Website:**  
https://digital-product-selling-platform.netlify.app/

---

## 📖 About The Project

**Digital Product Hub** is a web-based digital product selling platform designed to connect digital product creators and buyers.

Creators can list their digital products with a title, description, category, preview image, pricing model, and downloadable files.

Buyers can browse products, search for products, add products to their cart, make payments, and access their purchased digital products.

The platform is designed with a simple, modern, and user-friendly interface.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Home Page

- Attractive landing page
- Digital Product Hub branding
- Product search
- Product categories
- Featured products
- Easy navigation

### 🛍️ Product Browsing

Users can:

- Browse digital products
- Search for products
- View product details
- View product prices
- View product preview images
- Explore products by category

### 📂 Product Categories

The platform supports:

- 🎨 UI Kits
- 📄 Templates
- 🔌 Plugins
- 📚 E-books
- 💻 Courses
- 📦 Digital Assets

### 🧑‍💻 Seller Features

Sellers can:

- Create a product listing
- Add product title
- Add product description
- Select a category
- Add preview image
- Set product price
- Select one-time purchase or subscription
- Upload digital product files
- Publish products
- Manage listed products
- Track sales and orders

### 🛒 Shopping Cart

Buyers can:

- Add products to cart
- View cart items
- Increase product quantity
- Decrease product quantity
- Remove products
- View total price
- Proceed to checkout

### 💳 Payment

The project includes payment functionality using:

- Razorpay
- Order creation
- Payment processing
- Payment confirmation

### 📥 Digital Product Delivery

After a successful purchase:

- The buyer can access the purchased product.
- Digital files can be provided for download.
- Products are intended for instant digital delivery.

### 👤 Authentication

The platform supports user authentication for accessing protected features.

### 📊 Order Management

The backend supports:

- Creating orders
- Viewing user orders
- Viewing seller orders
- Viewing individual order details
- Updating order status
- Tracking downloads
